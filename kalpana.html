MISSION

KALPANA

Named in honour of Kalpana Chawla India's first woman in space Mission Kalpana is STARC's next high-power rocketry mission, pushing faster and higher with a passively stabilised architecture.

Vehicle ITR-1 uses fixed fins tuned for a 1.64-caliber stability margin. No servo actuation. Powered by the K1800ST motor to Mach 0.511 and 1075 m apogee.

// VEHICLE ITR-1

Vehicle Design

ITR-1 is 175 cm long with a 13 cm max diameter, built around PVC airframe tubes with aluminum couplers. Unlike Vajra, there are no servo-actuated canards stability is achieved entirely through fixed carbon fiber fins sized to produce a 1.64-caliber margin.

The K1800ST motor drives the vehicle to peak Mach 0.511 and 174 m/s. CG sits at 123 cm, CP at 144 cm. The result is a fast, high-flying vehicle that proves passive stability is engineering, not compromise.
